# Ligand–Receptor Compatibility Analysis — Results

## 1. Overview

This directory contains the results generated by `GitHub8_Paper1_ligand_receptor_compatibility_analysis.R`, which implements the ligand–receptor (LR) expression-compatibility workflow for the LUAD study.

**Study title:**\
*Integrated transcriptomic and single-cell characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R in LUAD.*

The workflow evaluates expression-based compatibility between predefined ligand–receptor records and previously generated single-cell pseudobulk expression profiles. It focuses on three cellular compartments:

- Endothelial cells
- Myeloid/macrophage cells (Mono/Macro)
- T cells (CD4Tconv, CD8T, and CD8Tex)

The analysis uses the predefined CCI_LR interaction resource and existing STEP10 single-cell expression outputs. It does not infer ligand–receptor pairs de novo from the expression data.

The results include input audits, patient- and sample-level cell-count assessments, expression and compatibility audits, genome-wide candidate screening, canonical patient-level integration, descriptive summaries, Figure 7 plotting outputs, and supplementary-table generation and traceability audits.

## 2. Scope and Interpretation

### 2.1 Analytical design

The workflow evaluates six predefined sender–receiver directions:

| Direction ID | Sender             | Receiver           |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| E_to_M       | Endothelial        | Myeloid/macrophage |
| M_to_E       | Myeloid/macrophage | Endothelial        |
| E_to_T       | Endothelial        | T cell             |
| T_to_E       | T cell             | Endothelial        |
| M_to_T       | Myeloid/macrophage | T cell             |
| T_to_M       | T cell             | Myeloid/macrophage |

The final matched-patient compatibility analyses use four patients represented across all three compartments.

The canonical patient-level analytical key is:

`Exact_LR_Pair × Direction_ID × Patient_Index`

The final descriptive master table contains one row per exact LR pair, while other outputs retain direction-specific and patient-specific records.

### 2.2 Expression compatibility

An LR record is considered expression-compatible in a given patient and direction when:

- The ligand satisfies the specified expression-detection rule in the sender compartment; and
- The corresponding receptor satisfies the same rule in the receiver compartment.

The workflow examines predefined detection rules, including raw UMI \> 0, CPM \> 0, and CPM ≥ 1, in the corresponding sensitivity analyses.

For the genome-wide patient-level screening workflow, the primary expression transformation is CPM followed by `log2(CPM + 1)`, with CPM \> 0 as the detection criterion. Patient-consistency summaries use the predefined thresholds of at least 2/4, at least 3/4, and 4/4 matched patients.

The stricter detection-rule analyses are sensitivity assessments. They should not be interpreted as independent validation.

### 2.3 Interpretation boundaries

The CCI_LR resource supplies predefined interaction records and resource-level annotations. The workflow evaluates expression compatibility against this reference; it does not perform de novo LR inference.

Expression compatibility does not establish:

- Direct molecular binding or physical interaction.
- Interaction specificity, strength, or communication probability.
- Functional cell–cell communication or pathway activation.
- Causal signaling or biological directionality.
- Experimental validation.

The workflow does not perform LR-specific statistical significance testing, P-value or FDR calculation, expression-matched permutation testing, background-null-model analysis, pathway enrichment, or biological candidate ranking.

Pathway and annotation summaries describe classifications inherited from the reference resource. They are not evidence of pathway activation or enrichment.

A `PASS` status indicates that the programmed structural, consistency, or traceability checks were satisfied. It does not indicate biological validation.

AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R are prioritized study genes and are assessed separately from the LR candidate interaction set. Their inclusion in the study does not imply that they form an LR signaling chain.

## 15. Final Interpretation

This module provides a structured, patient-level characterization of expression-based ligand–receptor compatibility across endothelial, myeloid/macrophage, and T-cell compartments in LUAD.

Its principal outputs are the locked candidate records, canonical patient-level integration table, descriptive patient- and direction-level summaries, Figure 7 panels, and manuscript-facing supplementary table.

The findings should be interpreted as computational evidence of ligand and receptor expression compatibility under predefined criteria. They do not demonstrate direct molecular interactions, communication probability, pathway activation, causal signaling, or experimental validation.

The analysis is restricted to four matched patients with representation of all three compartments. The observed recurrence patterns therefore describe this matched subset and should not be generalized as population-level validation.

No expression-matched permutation or background-null-model analysis was performed. Accordingly, the retained candidate set does not establish that the observed compatibility exceeds an appropriate background expectation.

The stricter detection-rule audits assess sensitivity to expression-detection definitions and should not be treated as independent biological validation.
