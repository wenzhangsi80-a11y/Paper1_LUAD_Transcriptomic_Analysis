# Ligand-Receptor Compatibility Analysis Results

## 1. Overview

This directory stores the output files generated by the ligand-receptor (LR) compatibility analysis workflow for the study:

**Integrated transcriptomic and single-cell characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R in LUAD**

The workflow evaluates expression-based ligand-receptor compatibility across three predefined cellular compartments:

- Endothelial cells
- Myeloid/macrophage cells
- T cells, including CD4Tconv, CD8T, and CD8Tex populations

Six predefined sender-receiver directions are considered:

1.  Endothelial → Myeloid/macrophage (E→M)
2.  Myeloid/macrophage → Endothelial (M→E)
3.  Endothelial → T cell (E→T)
4.  T cell → Endothelial (T→E)
5.  Myeloid/macrophage → T cell (M→T)
6.  T cell → Myeloid/macrophage (T→M)

The workflow integrates the predefined CCI_LR reference resource with existing STEP10 single-cell expression and pseudobulk outputs. It evaluates whether ligand and receptor genes are detected in the corresponding sender and receiver compartments within the same patient.

The downstream patient-level compatibility analyses use four matched patients with all three target compartments represented.

### Scope and interpretation

The outputs document:

- Input-resource and cell-metadata audits.
- Patient-level and sample-level cell-count distributions.
- Core-gene cellular localization.
- Compatibility between the CCI_LR resource and the STEP10 expression data.
- Detection-rule sensitivity and patient-level compatibility.
- Structural characterization of candidate LR records.
- Candidate-set provenance and canonical patient-level integration.
- Descriptive expression and compatibility summaries.
- Figure 7 and Supplementary Table Sx generation and traceability audits.

These analyses are expression-based compatibility assessments using a predefined reference resource. They do not constitute de novo ligand-receptor discovery or direct experimental measurement of cell-cell communication.

In particular, compatibility does not establish direct molecular interaction, interaction strength, communication probability, pathway activation, causal signaling, or experimental validation.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, summaries are descriptive. The workflow does not generate statistical significance claims, P values, FDR values, pathway enrichment results, or biological candidate rankings.
