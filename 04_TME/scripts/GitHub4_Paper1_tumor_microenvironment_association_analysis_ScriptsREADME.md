# Tumor Microenvironment Analysis Scripts

## Study

Integrated transcriptomic and single-cell characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R in LUAD.

## 1. Overview

This directory contains the R scripts used for tumor microenvironment (TME) association analyses and related sensitivity analyses in the LUAD transcriptomic study.

The workflow includes three complementary R scripts:

1.  `GitHub4_Paper1_tumor_microenvironment_association_analysis_1.R`
2.  `GitHub4_Paper1_tumor_microenvironment_association_analysis_2.R`
3.  `Supplementary_Table_ABSOLUTE_Purity_Adjusted_Regression.R`

The first script performs the original gene–TME association analysis, prepares manuscript-related tables and figures, audits the ABSOLUTE tumor-purity source, conducts purity-adjusted regression analyses, and investigates the provenance of historical ESTIMATE inputs and signatures.

The second script focuses on ESTIMATE implementation recovery and validation, reconstruction of the original scores, signature-substitution validation, and formal leave-one-gene-out (LOO) sensitivity analyses.

The third script extracts and audits the archived ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted regression results generated by the preceding STEP7B5 and STEP7B6 workflows. It prepares Supplementary Table 3a and supporting audit files without refitting the archived regression models.

Together, these scripts document the original gene–TME association analysis, tumor-purity-adjusted regression analysis, and ESTIMATE signature-removal sensitivity analyses.

The three prioritized study genes — AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R — were carried forward from preceding exploratory bulk-transcriptomic analyses. They should not be interpreted as the initial DEG-ranking results or as PPI hub genes.

- ImmuneScore

Adjustment covariate:

The analysis covers nine prespecified gene–TME combinations (3 genes × 3 TME scores).

### 5.6 Sample and Model Criteria

The script verifies the following expected conditions:

- 500 samples in the locked STEP7B5 purity-adjusted dataset.
- Unique and non-missing sample identifiers.
- Complete gene expression, TME score, and ABSOLUTE purity measurements.
- Nine archived linear regression models.
- 500 observations in each model.
- Valid gene and purity coefficient estimates and associated statistics.

The 500-sample dataset is inherited from the upstream STEP7B5 workflow. The extraction script verifies the archived dataset rather than independently repeating the original sample-matching procedure.

### 5.7 Statistical Methods and Verification

The script performs:

1.  Loading the archived STEP7B5 and STEP7B6 RData files into isolated environments.
2.  Verification of the locked dataset structure and required variables.
3.  Verification of the expected gene–TME model combinations.
4.  Direct extraction of gene and ABSOLUTE purity coefficients from the archived `lm` models.
5.  Separation of gene-level and purity-covariate coefficient results.
6.  Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) adjustment across the nine gene-term P values.
7.  Comparison of the recalculated BH-adjusted results with the archived STEP7B6 results.
8.  Dataset, coefficient, and model audits.
9.  Generation of the publication-ready supplementary table.
10. Export of CSV files and an optional Excel workbook.
11. Generation of a final audit report.

No regression models are refitted. No sample rematching, barcode remapping, or purity imputation is performed.

The final audit includes a script-defined requirement that all nine gene-term BH-adjusted P values meet the prespecified FDR threshold. Actual audit completion and statistical significance must be confirmed from the generated outputs rather than inferred from the code alone.

## 8. Analysis Scope and Interpretation

The TME workflow addresses three related but distinct analytical questions:

1.  **Original gene–TME associations:** How are AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R expression associated with the original ESTIMATE-derived StromalScore, ImmuneScore, and ESTIMATEScore?
2.  **Tumor-purity-adjusted associations:** How are the gene–TME associations represented in regression models adjusted for ABSOLUTE tumor purity?
3.  **Signature-removal sensitivity:** How sensitive are the AOC3–StromalScore and IL7R–ImmuneScore associations to removal of the corresponding gene from its ESTIMATE signature?

The formal LOO analyses are restricted to:

- AOC3: removal from the StromalSignature.
- IL7R: removal from the ImmuneSignature.
- MS4A7: no LOO analysis, because it is not included in either original ESTIMATE signature.

These analyses evaluate associations with bulk-tissue transcriptional scores and the sensitivity of those scores to signature-gene removal. They do not directly measure individual immune-cell populations, establish cellular origin, demonstrate independent biological mechanisms, or prove causal regulation.

The purity-adjusted regression analysis is observational and should not be interpreted as demonstrating causal effects.

Signature-removal analyses address the direct inclusion of the corresponding gene in the relevant scoring signature, but do not eliminate all potential dependencies or confounding associated with bulk-tissue composition.

## 9. Reproducibility Notes

1.  Use the project setup and project-relative path configuration defined in the scripts.
2.  Execute scripts in accordance with their data and object dependencies.
3.  Verify the availability and structure of upstream WGCNA, ESTIMATE, ABSOLUTE, and Step14 inputs before running dependent analyses.
4.  Preserve the original locked WGCNA, ESTIMATE, and gene–TME source objects.
5.  Distinguish the primary 513-sample TME association cohort from the separate 500-sample purity-adjusted analysis dataset.
6.  Verify the actual R and package versions from the execution environment.
7.  Confirm sample identifiers, sample ordering, numerical completeness, and checkpoint readback.
8.  Distinguish script-defined validation criteria from successfully completed runtime checks.
9.  Do not interpret audit messages or the presence of PASS conditions in code as proof of successful execution without checking the corresponding output objects and files.
10. Do not describe figure construction or supplementary table formatting as new statistical analyses.
11. Do not interpret gene–TME associations or purity-adjusted regression coefficients as evidence of causality.
12. Do not infer a gene's quantitative contribution to an ESTIMATE score from signature membership alone.
13. Confirm the actual LOO score comparisons, association statistics, signature restoration, and final checkpoint status from the execution records.
14. For the supplementary ABSOLUTE extraction workflow, verify both archived RData inputs and all generated audit files.
15. Confirm the actual final audit status before treating Supplementary Table 3a as verified.

Repository revisions are restricted to project-relative path construction and standardized English comments. The original analysis logic, statistical procedures, thresholds, object definitions, and execution order are retained.
