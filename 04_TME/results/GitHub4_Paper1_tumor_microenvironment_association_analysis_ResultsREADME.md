# Tumor Microenvironment Analysis Results

## Study

Integrated transcriptomic and single-cell characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R in LUAD.

## 1. Overview

This directory documents the analytical outputs generated by the three Paper 1 tumor microenvironment (TME) analysis scripts:

- `GitHub4_Paper1_tumor_microenvironment_association_analysis_1.R`

- `GitHub4_Paper1_tumor_microenvironment_association_analysis_2.R`

- `Supplementary_Table_ABSOLUTE_Purity_Adjusted_Regression.R`

The TME analysis investigates the associations of three prioritized study genes — AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R — with ESTIMATE-derived TME scores in primary lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) samples.

The workflow also documents the source integrity of the original ESTIMATE scores, ABSOLUTE tumor-purity data, ESTIMATE signature membership, and the sensitivity of selected ESTIMATE scores to the removal of individual signature genes.

The results are organized into six major analytical components:

1.  Original gene–TME association analysis and supplementary table generation.

2.  ABSOLUTE tumor-purity source audits and purity-adjusted regression model fitting.

3.  Archived ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted regression result extraction and verification.

4.  Historical ESTIMATE source and signature-membership audits.

5.  Original ESTIMATE score reconstruction and official implementation validation.

6.  Formal leave-one-gene-out (LOO) sensitivity analyses for AOC3 and IL7R.

The primary gene–TME association analysis and the formal LOO sensitivity analyses are based on the 513 primary LUAD samples represented in their corresponding locked upstream data objects.

The ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted regression analysis uses the separate locked STEP7B5 dataset, which is expected to contain 500 samples. This dataset is distinct from the original 513-sample gene–TME analysis cohort.

The supplementary extraction script reads the archived STEP7B5 dataset and STEP7B6 fitted models. It extracts and audits the archived regression coefficients and prepares Supplementary Table 3a without refitting the models.

This README summarizes the analytical outputs, expected files, and validation framework. Actual numerical estimates, sample counts, file-generation status, and audit outcomes must be confirmed using the corresponding execution logs and saved output files.

## 9. Interpretation and Reporting Limitations

The TME analyses are designed to characterize observational associations between the prioritized study genes and ESTIMATE-derived TME scores, evaluate the additional contribution of ABSOLUTE tumor purity in regression models, and assess the sensitivity of selected ESTIMATE scores to signature-gene removal.

The following distinctions should be maintained when interpreting and reporting the results:

1.  Gene–TME correlations describe statistical associations and do not establish causal relationships.

2.  Purity-adjusted regression estimates represent associations conditional on the included ABSOLUTE purity covariate; they do not establish causal effects.

3.  ESTIMATE signature membership indicates inclusion in a predefined gene set, not the quantitative contribution of a gene to the resulting score.

4.  AOC3-LOO and IL7R-LOO evaluate the sensitivity of their respective signature-derived scores to individual gene removal.

5.  MS4A7 is not included in either original ESTIMATE signature and is therefore not subjected to a signature-specific LOO analysis.

6.  The LOO analyses do not independently establish cell-type-specific functions or biological mechanisms.

7.  Reconstruction and signature-substitution validation results should be interpreted according to their explicit numerical criteria and audit definitions.

8.  Expected sample dimensions, signature sizes, numerical thresholds, and PASS indicators should not be presented as confirmed results without execution evidence.

9.  The original gene–TME association analysis uses the 513-sample primary LUAD cohort, whereas the ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted regression analysis uses the separate 500-sample STEP7B5 dataset.

10. Supplementary Table 3a reports archived ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted regression results and must be distinguished from the original ESTIMATE-based gene–TME correlation table.

11. The STEP7B6_05 extraction workflow does not refit the archived regression models or repeat sample matching, barcode remapping, or purity imputation.

12. The script-defined requirement that all nine gene-level BH-adjusted P values be below 0.05 is an audit condition. Its successful fulfillment must be confirmed from the actual output files and final audit report.

## 10. Reproducibility and Output Integrity

The following principles apply to the interpretation and preservation of the TME analysis outputs:

- Preserve the locked upstream WGCNA, ESTIMATE, and Step14 source objects.

- Distinguish the primary gene–TME association dataset from the separate ABSOLUTE purity-adjusted dataset.

- Retain original unrounded statistical values separately from formatted manuscript tables.

- Preserve the original ESTIMATE score reference when evaluating reconstructed or LOO scores.

- Verify sample identity and ordering when comparing expression matrices and score matrices.

- Distinguish persistent RData and GCT files from intermediate in-memory objects and console-level audit reports.

- Confirm the final contents of checkpoints after subsequent save or overwrite operations.

- Confirm actual output paths and file existence before final repository release.

- Record the actual R and package versions from the execution environment.

- Report numerical results and PASS/FAIL indicators only when supported by the corresponding execution logs and saved files.

- Verify the two archived STEP7B5 and STEP7B6 input files before running the supplementary extraction script.

- Confirm the integrity of the extracted coefficient table and the comparison with archived BH-adjusted results.

- Confirm the final status in `STEP7B6_05_Final_Audit.txt` before treating Supplementary Table 3a as verified.
