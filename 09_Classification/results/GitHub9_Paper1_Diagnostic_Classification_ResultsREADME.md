# Diagnostic Classification — Results

## 1. Overview

This directory stores the outputs generated by the LUAD diagnostic-classification workflow, including data-provenance audits, historical input reconciliation, model-definition locks, internal cross-validation assessment, final TCGA model fitting, external assessment using GSE32863, paired gene-expression comparisons, and figure generation.

The analyses focus on the exploratory characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R in tumor-versus-normal tissue discrimination.

The main workflow output directory is:

`WORKFLOW13_Diagnostic_Classification/`

The results are organized according to the major analytical stages:

1.  Configuration and interpretation locks.
2.  TCGA sample and historical cohort provenance audits.
3.  Historical Xiangtao ROC data traceability.
4.  GSE32863 historical object, expression, and sample-annotation audits.
5.  GSE32863 patient/subject pairing and final input lock.
6.  Historical expression-scale and cross-platform compatibility audits.
7.  Model-definition lock and internal cross-validation assessment.
8.  Final TCGA model fitting.
9.  GSE32863 external assessment and paired expression analysis.
10. Final manuscript result lock and figure generation.

The output files document the provenance, structure, and results of the specified workflow stages. Their presence does not automatically establish that all upstream checks passed or that every subsequent analysis was completed.

## 15. Reproducibility and Overall Interpretation

The classification workflow uses project-relative paths where implemented, with `here::here()` used to resolve project locations in the relevant modules.

Historical RData objects are loaded into isolated environments in the provenance-audit workflows where specified, reducing the risk of overwriting objects in the active R session.

R session information is recorded by the corresponding workflow stages to support documentation of the computational environment.

The results should be interpreted within the following boundaries:

1.  The historical Xiangtao-derived TCGA ROC branch and the current harmonized TCGA analysis branch are distinct data-provenance branches.
2.  The historical 516 Tumor + 59 Normal sample structure is not interchangeable with the current 513 Primary Tumor + 59 Normal + 2 Metastatic expression-sample structure.
3.  The current classification universe contains 513 Primary Tumor and 59 Solid Tissue Normal samples, totaling 572 eligible samples.
4.  The three study genes are AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R; the workflow evaluates their combined exploratory tissue-discrimination performance without implying a causal mechanism.
5.  STEP13G provides an internal outer-fold resampling assessment, not independent external validation or a fully executed nested cross-validation procedure.
6.  STEP13H fits the final model using the historical TCGA ROC branch, and STEP13I transfers that locked model to GSE32863 without external refitting.
7.  The GSE32863 external assessment and paired-expression analysis provide exploratory evidence and do not establish clinical diagnostic utility.
8.  Metadata-derived sample-code or subject pairing structures should not be interpreted beyond the level of evidence established by the corresponding GEO metadata audits.
9.  PASS markers represent predefined workflow-specific checks and should not be interpreted as universal validation of the full analytical pipeline.

Overall, this directory preserves the audit records, locked model definitions, internal and external assessment outputs, paired-expression results, and visualization files generated by the LUAD diagnostic-classification workflow. The analyses support exploratory transcriptomic characterization of AOC3, MS4A7, and IL7R across tumor and normal tissue samples, rather than a claim of clinical diagnostic validation.
